# RBG Labs

RBG Labs is a collaborative research group in the Department of Biology at Mount Allison University, bringing together the Riley Integrative Ecology Lab (RIEL) and the Anthropogenic Change Ecology (ACE) Lab. We study behavioural ecology, evolutionary biology, and the ecology and conservation of amphibians and reptiles.

Learn more about our research and team at the [RBG Labs Website](https://www.rbglabs.ca) or follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/rbglabs/).

## Software & Tools

R packages and reusable software developed by lab members.

- **[bodycon]([https://github.com/julia-riley/bodycon](https://github.com/RBGLabs/bodycon))** — An R package for estimating animal body condition in ecological research. [Archived release on Zenodo](https://zenodo.org/records/23066175).

## Research Projects

Data, code, and reproducible workflows using Git associated with our research projects and publications.

- **[The genetic origin of salamanders introduced to Newfoundland](https://github.com/yang1132/yang-et-al-pcinereus-nfld-genetics/tree/main)** | Zenodo repository | GenBank sequences: A study investigating where Eastern Red-backed Salamanders were introduced to Newfoundland from using genetic methods.
- **[Impact of moisture on reptile developmental plasticity](https://github.com/camtbell/repdevomoisture)** | [OSF project](https://osf.io/3ptcz/overview) | [Publication (2025)](https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/228/4/JEB249960/367049): A meta-analysis examining relationships between soil moisture levels and oviparous reptile developmental plasticity.
